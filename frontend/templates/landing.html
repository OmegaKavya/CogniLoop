The Problem We Solve

YouTube Has the Content.
CogniLoop Adds the Intelligence.

Watching lecture videos on YouTube is passive. Students skip, zone out, and move on without knowing what they missed. CogniLoop wraps existing educational video content in an active learning layer: one that watches how you watch, identifies gaps, and intervenes with the right question at the right time.

How It Works

Three Steps to Mastery

CogniLoop's adaptive loop continuously refines your learning path based on real behavioral signals.

1

Watch & Engage

Study curated video lectures with embedded concept checkpoints. The system tracks your pauses, rewinds, and speed, building a micro-behavioral profile.

2

Adaptive Quiz

After each submodule, take an AI-generated quiz. Question difficulty is selected by a Thompson Sampling bandit conditioned on your BKT mastery state.

3

Master & Grow

Get AI-powered insights, personalized cheat sheets, focus concepts, and resource recommendations. Track your Normalized Learning Gain over time.

Under the Hood

Research-Backed Algorithms

Every feature in CogniLoop is grounded in published educational data mining and intelligent tutoring research.

Bayesian Knowledge Tracing

Models each concept's mastery probability using a Hidden Markov Model with four parameters: P(L₀), P(T), P(G), P(S). Updates after every response.

Thompson Sampling Bandit

Selects question difficulty via a contextual multi-armed bandit policy. Each arm (easy/medium/hard) maintains Beta distributions updated by learning outcomes.

RAG-Powered Quiz Generation

Retrieval-Augmented Generation extracts transcript context from watched video segments. Groq LLM generates curriculum-aligned MCQs grounded in lecture content.

Micro-Pattern Analytics

Tracks pause, rewind, speed-change, and skip events during video playback. Classifies learner pace (Fast/Standard/Slow) to contextualise quiz difficulty selection.

Secure Authentication

PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 password hashing with 100K iterations, 16-byte random salts, and timing-attack resistant comparison via secrets.compare_digest.

Normalized Learning Gain

Measures actual learning effectiveness using (post − pre) / (1 − pre). Validated under simulated N=60 student profiles with p<0.05 statistical significance.

Evidence of Learning

How CogniLoop Helps You Grow: With Proof

Every interaction generates measurable evidence of learning progression. Here's how the system proves you're improving.

01

Mastery Tracking, Not Just Scores

Traditional platforms give you a percentage. CogniLoop tracks concept-level mastery using Bayesian Knowledge Tracing. You don't just see "75%": you see that you've mastered linked lists but still need work on AVL rotations.

02

Adaptive Difficulty = Optimal Challenge

Questions that are too easy don't teach. Too hard questions frustrate. CogniLoop's Thompson Sampling bandit finds the Zone of Proximal Development, giving you questions just hard enough to push growth, backed by learning science.

03

Normalized Learning Gain

We measure real learning using NLG = (post - pre) / (1 - pre), the gold standard metric in education research. Our evaluation shows statistically significant improvement (p<0.05) compared to static sequential instruction.

04

AI Insights & Personalized Cheat Sheets

After every quiz, get an AI-generated analysis of your weak areas, focus concepts, actionable cheat sheets, and curated resource links. No generic "study more": specific, evidence-based guidance.

05

Behavioral Learning Profile

The micro-pattern engine tracks how you learn: your pause-rewind patterns, speed preferences, and engagement signals. This builds a behavioral profile that adapts the entire experience to your learning style.

06

Visible Progress Dashboard

Interactive dashboards show mastery charts, heatmaps, streaks, XP, and attempt history. You can see yourself improving over time: concrete proof of growth, not just intuition.
